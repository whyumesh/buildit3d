# Builit3D Landing Page Design Guidelines

## Design Approach
**Reference-Based Approach**: Drawing inspiration from modern tech service companies like Stripe, Figma, and Linear for clean, minimalistic aesthetics with strategic use of animation.

## Core Design Elements

### Color Palette
- **Primary**: Deep tech blue (220 85% 25%) for trust and precision
- **Secondary**: Warm gray (210 15% 20%) for text and subtle elements
- **Accent**: Vibrant cyan (185 100% 60%) sparingly used for CTAs and key highlights
- **Background**: Clean whites (0 0% 98%) with subtle gray sections (210 20% 96%)
- **Gradients**: Subtle blue-to-cyan gradients (220 85% 25% to 185 100% 60%) for hero backgrounds and CTAs

### Typography
- **Primary**: Inter or Poppins from Google Fonts for clean, modern readability
- **Hierarchy**: 
  - Hero headline: 3xl-4xl, bold weight
  - Section headers: xl-2xl, semibold
  - Body text: base-lg, regular weight
  - Captions: sm, medium weight

### Layout System
- **Spacing**: Consistent use of Tailwind units 4, 8, 12, and 16 for harmonious proportions
- **Grid**: Max-width containers with generous padding (px-4 to px-8)
- **Sections**: Clear vertical rhythm with py-16 to py-24 spacing

### Component Library
- **Buttons**: Primary solid buttons with hover lift effects, outline variants with blurred backgrounds for hero overlays
- **Cards**: Subtle shadows with rounded corners (rounded-xl) and hover elevations
- **Navigation**: Clean horizontal nav with smooth transitions
- **Forms**: Minimalist inputs with focus states and validation feedback

### Animations
- **Page Load**: Subtle fade-in animations (0.6s duration) for content sections
- **Scroll Triggers**: Elements animate into view with slight upward motion
- **Hover Effects**: Gentle scale transforms (scale-105) on interactive elements
- **Micro-interactions**: Button press feedback and form input focus animations
- **Hero**: Floating geometric shapes or 3D model wireframes with slow rotation

## Landing Page Structure (Maximum 4 Sections)

1. **Hero Section**: Full viewport with Builit3D logo, compelling tagline about precision 3D printing, primary CTA, and animated background elements
2. **Services Showcase**: Grid of 3D printing services (prototyping, production, materials) with hover animations
3. **Portfolio Gallery**: Selected high-quality 3D printed projects with smooth image transitions
4. **Contact/CTA**: Simple contact form with company info and final conversion focus

## Images
- **Hero Image**: Large background showcasing precision 3D printer in action or geometric wireframe overlay
- **Service Icons**: Clean, minimalist illustrations of 3D printing processes
- **Portfolio Images**: High-quality photos of finished 3D printed products with professional lighting
- **Background Elements**: Subtle geometric patterns or 3D wireframe graphics

## Key Principles
- **White Space**: Generous spacing between elements for breathing room
- **Hierarchy**: Clear visual progression guiding users through the conversion funnel
- **Performance**: Optimized animations that enhance rather than distract
- **Focus**: Single primary goal - converting visitors to 3D printing service inquiries
